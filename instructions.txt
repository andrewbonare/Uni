 
 Person Class (Base Class):
Attributes: Name, Age, Address.
Methods: Constructor, getters, sette




Student Class (Inherits from Person):
Attributes: Student ID, Major, GPA.
Methods: Enroll in courses, drop courses, check grades.




Professor Class (Inherits from Person):
Attributes: Employee ID, Department, List of courses taught.
Methods: Add grades to students, update course materials.
Course Class:
Attributes: Course code, Title, Professor (instance of Professor class), List of enrolled students.
Methods: Add/remove students, calculate average grades.
Department Class:
Attributes: Department
Department Class:
Attributes: Department name, List of professors, List of courses offered.
Methods: Add/remove professors, add/remove courses.
University Class:
Attributes: List of departments, List of students, List of professors.
Methods: Enroll new students, hire new professors.
TESTING
Test Student Enrollment:
Test enrolling a student in a course and verify that the student is added to the list of enrolled students for that course.
Test enrolling a student in multiple courses and verify that the student is correctly enrolled in all courses.
Test Professor Assigning Grades:
Test a professor assigning grades to a student for a course and verify that the grade is correctly recorded for that student in the course.
Test assigning grades to multiple students for a course and verify that all grades are recorded accurately.
est Course Average Grade Calculation:
Test calculating the average grade for a course with multiple enrolled students and verify that the average grade is computed correctly.
Test Department Adding/Removing Professors:
Test adding a professor to a department and verify that the professor is added to the list of professors in the department.
Test removing a professor from a department and verify that the professor is removed from the list of professors in the department.
